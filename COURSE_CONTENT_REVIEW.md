# Course Content Review

**Course:** Women’s Safety in the AI Era — Basic Foundation Course  
**Institution:** Atlas AI Institute

## Coverage verified by static inspection

The data file contains all 8 approved modules, 40 bilingual lesson title/body pairs (five lessons per module, including one added supplemental lesson in each chapter), 24 module quiz questions, and paired English/Bengali fields for introductions, activities, summaries, options and explanations. The curriculum covers AI-era safety, AI-enabled gender-based violence, deepfakes and impersonation, cybercrime and sextortion, misinformation, digital rights and privacy, support/evidence/reporting, and practical digital safety.

## Instructional observations

The content is beginner-oriented, uses fictional examples and generally avoids victim-blaming. It repeatedly tells learners they do not need to disclose personal abuse experiences. It distinguishes platform takedown from official reporting and avoids promising a legal outcome. The bilingual presentation and language-focus control are useful for mixed-language learners.

## Editorial approval still required

Static field checks cannot judge Bengali naturalness, terminology consistency, trauma-informed tone or factual accuracy. A native Bengali editor, safeguarding specialist and Bangladesh-based legal/reporting reviewer must approve the course before public publication. All current contact numbers, reporting routes and legal references should be re-verified immediately before launch because these details can change.

## Quality findings

No duplicate module IDs were found. The product has a strong content base, but the current LMS does not yet implement the final assessment specified by the course brief. Therefore content completeness is acceptable for the module curriculum, while completion and certification readiness are not yet acceptable for launch.
